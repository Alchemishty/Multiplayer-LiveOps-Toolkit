# Third-party content

Unity package dependencies are pinned in `Packages/manifest.json` and `Packages/packages-lock.json`. Their distributions contain the applicable licenses and notices. Backend dependency versions are listed in `backend/requirements.txt`.

The project also contains imported TextMesh Pro essentials under `Assets/TextMesh Pro/`. Liberation Sans includes its font license at `Assets/TextMesh Pro/Fonts/LiberationSans - OFL.txt`; preserve that notice.

The character FBX and Imphenzia textures/materials under `Assets/Models/` are demo art. Their redistribution license is not recorded in this repository. This private snapshot does not establish permission to redistribute them as part of another product.
